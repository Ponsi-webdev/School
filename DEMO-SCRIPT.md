# Five-minute demonstration script

1. **Home page** (laptop): point out the demo notice, the contact bar (placeholders), the hero (photo slot), programmes, news and events from the dashboard's data.
2. **Admissions**: requirements, steps, dates "to be confirmed", FAQ, downloads "not yet available". Say clearly it is an enquiry form, not online application.
3. **Mobile**: open the same site on a phone (or narrow the window to 390 px). Show the Menu button, one-column layout and the enquiry form.
4. **Admin sign-in**: open `/admin/`. Say clearly this demo sign-in has no passwords and is not secure; the real version uses proper accounts.
5. **Create an announcement**: sign in as the Editor. News > Write news > fill in > Save draft > open the public News page to show it is NOT there.
6. **Publish**: Preview, then Publish. Refresh the public News page and the home page: the announcement appears.
7. **Unpublish** to show it disappears, and **Staff access** (as the Administrator) to show roles.
Close by explaining what the paid project adds: real photos, confirmed content, a connected database with real sign-in, and training.
